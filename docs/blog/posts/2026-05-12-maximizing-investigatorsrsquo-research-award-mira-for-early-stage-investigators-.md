# Maximizing Investigators&rsquo; Research Award (MIRA) for Early Stage Investigators (ESI)

NIH's MIRA (R35) mechanism for Early Stage Investigators — a broad,
single-award program that funds a PI's research program rather than a
single project. Worth flagging for Emory early-stage faculty in
pharmacoepidemiology and comparative effectiveness research.
