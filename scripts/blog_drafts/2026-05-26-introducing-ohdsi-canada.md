# Introducing OHDSI Canada

**Source**: [OHDSI Forums](https://forums.ohdsi.org/t/introducing-ohdsi-canada/25412)
**Matched keywords**: OHDSI

OHDSI Canada is now officially part of the global OHDSI community!
Monthly MS Teams meetings will be held on the second Wednesday of each month from 2:00 –3:00 p.m. ET, with the first meeting scheduled for June 10, 2026.
To join the group, access the MS Teams channel, receive monthly meeting invitations, or be added to the mailing list, please complete this form or contact archbold@ohdsi.org.
