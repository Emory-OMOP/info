# Independent Researcher Exploring Anxiety, Autonomic Regulation, and OMOP

**Source**: [OHDSI Forums](https://forums.ohdsi.org/t/independent-researcher-exploring-anxiety-autonomic-regulation-and-omop/26415)
**Matched keywords**: OMOP, OHDSI

Hello OHDSI community! I’m an independent researcher studying how heart rate variability (HRV), physiological state transitions, and cognitive demands relate to anxiety and performance. I’ve analyzed WESAD, Southampton, and OpenNeuro data and am now testing a MIMIC-IV to OMOP pipeline in PostgreSQL. I’d welcome feedback on OMOP mapping, biomarker representation, methods, and potential collaboration. My HC/SM model is a hypothesis, not an established finding. Glad to be here!
