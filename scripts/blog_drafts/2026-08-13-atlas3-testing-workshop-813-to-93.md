# Atlas3 Testing Workshop 8/13 to 9/3

**Source**: [OHDSI Forums](https://forums.ohdsi.org/t/atlas3-testing-workshop-8-13-to-9-3/25776)
**Matched keywords**: OHDSI

Greetings, community!
The Atlas WG will be hosting an Atlas3 Testing Workshop from 2pm to 4pm EST.
Information:
This Atlas3 Workshop series will be held 2pm - 4pm EST every Thursday from 8/13 to 9/3. The purpose of this workshop is to bring testers together to exercise the application and report issues. Starting on 8/13, we will gather and report issues in github, and the subsequent week we will repeat the exercise on a updated version with the prior-week’s fixes.
