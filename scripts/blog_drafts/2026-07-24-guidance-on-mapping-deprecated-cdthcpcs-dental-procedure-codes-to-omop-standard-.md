# Guidance on mapping deprecated CDT/HCPCS dental procedure codes to OMOP standard concepts

**Source**: [OHDSI Forums](https://forums.ohdsi.org/t/guidance-on-mapping-deprecated-cdt-hcpcs-dental-procedure-codes-to-omop-standard-concepts/25709)
**Matched keywords**: OMOP, OHDSI

Hi OHDSI community,
We are working on extracting dental procedure data from Epic Clarity/Wisdom into OMOP CDM and are reviewing how to map CDT/HCPCS dental procedure codes to OMOP standard concepts.
One issue we are seeing is that some dental procedure codes appear in Athena as deprecated/non-standard concepts and do not have a visible Maps to standard concept.
For example:
D0120 — Periodic oral evaluation - established patient
Athena concept: Athena
In Athena, this appears as a deprecated HCPCS
