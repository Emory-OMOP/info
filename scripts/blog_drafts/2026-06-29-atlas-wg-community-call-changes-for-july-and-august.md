# Atlas WG Community call changes for July and August

**Source**: [OHDSI Forums](https://forums.ohdsi.org/t/atlas-wg-community-call-changes-for-july-and-august/25560)
**Matched keywords**: OHDSI

Hello OHDSI Community:
I wanted to alert members of the AtlasWG that we will be moving the July and August meetings from the first Thursday to the second Thursday of the month at the same time (10am-11am EST).  Hope to see you all there as we do a deeper dive into the new UI and other technical topics.
