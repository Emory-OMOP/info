PROJECT SUMMARY

receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs) for the prevention of cardiovascular disease among individuals with
cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome. Although randomized controlled trials have demonstrated the
benefits of GLP-1RAs for weight management and glycemic control, there is less evidence on their role in
